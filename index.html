Voice Studio

Quiet room, phone on silent, tablet 20–30 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side. Each line now has a pace, an emotion and a delivery note. Read it like you are telling someone, not reading word by word. If you have an examples pack, tap Example first and copy its rhythm.

Wait a beat after Record, and hold still with your mouth relaxed for half a second after the last word before you tap Stop.
